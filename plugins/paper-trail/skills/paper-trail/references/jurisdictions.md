# Jurisdiction checklist

What each place commonly adds to the pages. This is a checklist of things to ask about and
include, written from general knowledge and not checked against current law in this session.
Every row ends in a **verify** note: put it in the report so the owner confirms it with an
official source or a lawyer before relying on it. Never write "we comply with X"; describe what
the product does.

| Where users are | Commonly adds | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU / EEA (GDPR) | legal basis per purpose; rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, port; right to complain to a supervisory authority; international transfer safeguards; consent before non-essential cookies (ePrivacy); a named controller and contact | the regulator's current guidance and whether an EU representative is required |
| United Kingdom (UK GDPR, PECR) | the same rights as the EU column; complaint to the ICO; cookie consent before non-essential cookies | ICO guidance for small organisations |
| California (CCPA/CPRA) | categories collected and disclosed; whether data is sold or shared; right to know, delete, correct and opt out; "Do Not Sell or Share" link if it applies; no discrimination for exercising rights | the current thresholds that decide whether the law applies to this business |
| Other US states with privacy laws | similar access, deletion and opt-out rights | which states' thresholds the business meets |
| India (DPDP Act) | notice of the data and purpose; consent; rights to access, correct and erase; grievance contact | the rules in force at launch |
| Canada (PIPEDA) | purposes identified at collection; consent; access and correction | provincial law (for example Quebec) |
| Australia (Privacy Act) | APP-style notice; access and correction; complaints | whether the small-business exemption applies |
| Brazil (LGPD) | legal basis; data subject rights; a contact for the data officer | current ANPD guidance |
| Consumers anywhere | statutory refund or cooling-off rights that a "no refunds" line cannot remove (EU 14-day withdrawal for many online sales, with a digital-content exception only when the user consents) | consumer law where the buyer lives |
| Payment providers | Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad and app stores impose their own refund and policy-page rules; a merchant of record may own the refund terms | the provider's current seller terms |

If the owner cannot say where users are, write the pages for the strictest likely set (EU rights,
cookie consent) and say so in the report.
