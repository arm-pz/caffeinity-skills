# Pages

## Which pages

| Page | Needed when | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy policy | any personal data at all: an email field, analytics, server logs with IPs | `privacy.md` |
| Terms of service | users create accounts, upload content, or pay | `terms.md` |
| Refund policy | the product charges money | `refunds.md` |
| Cookie notice | any non-essential cookie or tracker (analytics, ads, embeds) | `cookies.md` |

A site with no forms, no analytics and no payments may need none of these; say so rather than
writing boilerplate.

## Anatomy

The checker requires the headings marked `*` (case-insensitive, any heading level). Add more
sections as the facts and jurisdictions require.

### Privacy policy

1. Who we are* (legal_name, contact_email, postal_address)
2. What we collect* (data_collected, one row or bullet per item, with its source)
3. Why we collect it* (purpose_of_each; legal_basis for EU/UK)
4. Who we share it with* (processors, by name and what each receives)
5. How long we keep it* (retention)
6. Your rights* (user_rights_process, plus jurisdiction rights)
7. Children (minimum_user_age)
8. Changes to this policy (changes_notice)
9. Contact* (contact_email)

### Terms of service

1. Agreement (who the user is agreeing with, effective_date)
2. The service* (what_the_product_does)
3. Accounts (if any)
4. Acceptable use* (acceptable_use)
5. Your content (user_content; only if users upload)
6. Payment (charges, billing; link the refund policy)
7. Termination* (account_termination)
8. Disclaimers and liability* (liability_cap)
9. Governing law* (governing_law, disputes)
10. Changes (changes_notice)
11. Contact* (contact_email)

### Refund policy

1. What you can get refunded* (refund_window, refund_conditions)
2. How to ask* (contact_email and what to include)
3. Subscriptions and cancellation (billing_cycle_and_renewal)
4. Exceptions (statutory rights the owner cannot remove)
5. Contact* (contact_email)

### Cookie notice

1. What cookies are (two sentences)
2. Cookies we use* (table: name, provider, purpose, duration, essential or not)
3. Your choices* (consent_tool, how to withdraw)
4. Contact* (contact_email)

## Footer

Each page is linked from every page footer. Checkout links the terms and refund policy; sign-up
and email forms link the privacy policy at the point of collection.
