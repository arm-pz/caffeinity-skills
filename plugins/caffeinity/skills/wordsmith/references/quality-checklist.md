# Quality checklist

Before responding, check the following.

## Task

- Did I understand what the user asked for?
- Did I choose the appropriate operation?
- Did I follow the requested format?
- Did I respect the requested length?

## Meaning

- Did I understand what the user asked for?
- Did I choose the appropriate operation?
- Did I choose the least invasive operation that satisfies the request?
- Did I preserve the intended meaning?
- Did I preserve important facts?
- Did I preserve names, numbers, dates, and quotations?
- Did I preserve necessary qualifications?
- Did I avoid changing commitments silently?

## Writing quality

- Is the main point clear?
- Is the structure logical?
- Are paragraphs focused?
- Are transitions useful?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Is the language specific?
- Is the wording concise?
- Is the rhythm natural?
- Is the ending effective?

## Accuracy

- Did I avoid inventing facts?
- Did I avoid inventing citations or sources?
- Did I distinguish fact from opinion?
- Did I flag claims that require verification?
- Did I avoid claiming that unverified information is correct?

## Voice

- Did I preserve the author's voice where requested?
- Did I avoid generic corporate language?
- Did I avoid making the writing unnaturally enthusiastic?
- Did I avoid making the writing sound more formal than requested?

## Accessibility

- Did I explain necessary jargon?
- Are sentences understandable?
- Are headings and lists useful?
- Did I avoid unnecessary complexity?
- Did I preserve important nuance?

## Response

- Did I return the requested writing before commentary?
- Did I explain only significant changes?
- Did I avoid unnecessary disclaimers?
- Did I ask a question only if it was genuinely necessary?
- Did I identify unresolved issues clearly?
