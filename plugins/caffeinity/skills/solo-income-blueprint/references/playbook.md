## Model comparison

| Model | Fits when | First revenue | Startup cost | Core risk | Validation test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freelance service | A marketable skill, cash needed soon | Days to 2 weeks | Near zero | Feast and famine, price competition | Ten targeted proposals or five conversations |
| Productized / done-for-you | One repeatable outcome, business buyers | 1-3 weeks | Near zero | Scope creep | One paid pilot |
| AI-assisted service | You can deliver an existing service faster | Days to 2 weeks | Low | Undifferentiated "AI" pitch | One paid pilot with a before/after |
| Digital product | Distribution exists or demand is searchable | 2-8 weeks | Low | No buyers | Pre-sale or ten-person waitlist |
| Course / cohort | Proven teaching, existing audience | 4-12 weeks | Low to medium | Recording before demand | Pre-sell before recording |
| Coaching / consulting | Credible results, can sell 1:1 | 1-4 weeks | Near zero | Personal network dependency | Five conversations, one paying client |
| Content monetization | Long horizon, enjoys publishing | 6-18 months | Near zero | Needs volume | Thirty-day publishing test |
| Affiliate | Can build an audience in a buying niche | 2-6 months | Low | Trust and disclosure | Ten useful pieces or a small audience test |
| Micro-SaaS | Technical, narrow validated pain, can support software | 2-6 months | Medium | Building before demand | Landing page plus ten conversations |

Heuristic: fastest path is selling a service you can already deliver; most durable is a
product with an owned audience. Cash urgency pushes toward services, hours push away
from software, and no proof pushes toward conversations before building.

## Interview follow-ups

- Skill: "What have people asked you to help with more than twice?"
- Interest: "What do you read or watch without being paid to?"
- Time: "Which days and hours, and is that predictable?"
- Budget: "What can you lose without it hurting?"
- Goal: "First payment, replacing income, or learning the game?"

## Demand checks

Choose one, matching the model:

- **Conversations:** five talks with the target buyer; evidence is three describing the
  problem without being prompted. A no is five conversations with no problem
  recognition.
- **Paid pilot:** one buyer pays a small deposit or a reduced first engagement.
- **Pre-sale:** the offer is sold before it is built; evidence is one payment.
- **Waitlist:** a one-page offer plus ten reachable signups from people in the niche.
- **Proposal test:** ten targeted proposals; evidence is a reply that discusses scope or
  price, not "sounds interesting".

Treat any threshold as directional, not a statistic. Never present it as a benchmark.

## Offer checklist

- **Buyer:** one sentence, and reachable this week.
- **Problem:** in their words, not yours.
- **Outcome:** concrete or measurable.
- **Mechanism:** how delivery works, in steps.
- **Price:** anchored to the cost of the problem or the alternative; start simple.
- **Guarantee:** refund or rework you can afford.
- **Differentiator:** speed, specificity, or proof.
- **Delivery:** steps, time, tools.
- **Proof:** sample, pilot result, or before/after.

## Content engine

Pillars: problem education, proof or demo, opinion or story. Pick three.

Hook formulas:

- "Most [audience] think X. The problem is Y."
- "I did X in Y. Here is the part nobody tells you."
- "Stop doing X if you want Y."

Platform fit:

| Platform | Best format | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | Short text, threads, replies | Requires daily presence to compound |
| LinkedIn | Stories and lessons, document posts | Professional tone, slower feedback |
| YouTube | Search-led demos and tutorials | Production time, thumbnail skill |
| TikTok / Reels | Short demos, before/after | Needs volume and speed |
| Newsletter | Weekly useful email | Needs an existing way to get subscribers |

Rule: one primary channel, three posts a week, for four weeks before judging.

## Sales path

**Lead magnet types:** checklist, template, short guide, calculator, teardown.

**Five-email sequence:**

1. Deliver the freebie and one quick win.
2. Story: why this problem matters and what usually goes wrong.
3. Objection: handle the most common reason not to buy.
4. Proof: pilot result, sample, or walkthrough.
5. Offer: what they get, price, guarantee, deadline you can honor.

**Sales page outline:** headline, problem, outcome, mechanism, proof, offer, price,
guarantee, FAQ, call to action.

**Outreach script:** specific observation about them, the problem it suggests, one
sentence of relevant proof, and a small ask. Follow up on day 3, day 7, and day 14,
then stop.

**Objections:** expensive → cost of the problem; no time → smallest first step; trust →
pilot or refund; do it myself → what the DIY version costs in hours.

## Automation map

Automate only what has already worked manually:

| Task | Automate with | Keep human |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research and source collection | AI summaries | Choosing the angle |
| First drafts | Templates plus AI | Voice and proof |
| Repurposing one post into a thread or email | AI rewrite | Final edit |
| Scheduling | Scheduler | Reply to comments |
| Lead capture | Landing page plus email tool | Segmentation judgment |
| Follow-up reminders | Email tool | Replies that need a human |
| FAQ support | AI draft replies | Refunds and complaints |
| Delivery | Templates and file links | Kickoff call |

## Tool stack

One tool per category, free tier first, paid only when the free tier blocks a result.

Categories: writing and drafts, design, landing page, email and lead capture, payments,
scheduling, automation, support, file delivery and calls.

When naming a tool, say why it fits and whether the free tier is confirmed this
session. Otherwise write "check current pricing". Do not list more than one option per
category unless the user asks for alternatives.

## Seven-day launch plan

| Day | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | One-page offer drafted; list of twenty reachable buyers |
| 2 | Five validation conversations sent or booked |
| 3 | Offer revised from responses; one landing page or profile CTA live |
| 4 | Three pieces of problem or proof content published |
| 5 | Ten personalized outreach messages sent |
| 6 | Follow-ups sent; first calls or sales conversations held |
| 7 | Evidence reviewed; continue, adjust, or stop; next week's cadence set |

If the user has fewer than five hours a week, spread the same seven outcomes over
fourteen days and say so. The plan launches the offer; it does not promise revenue.

## Anti-patterns to refuse

- A model chosen because a guru sells a course about it.
- Building a product before any buyer conversation.
- Launching on five platforms at once.
- Fake scarcity, fake results, income guarantees, or undisclosed affiliate and
  sponsorship relationships.
- Paid ads before organic proof or a converting offer.
- Complicated funnels for a first sale.
- Requests for passwords, keys, or account access.
